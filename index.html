TASK 1001: Support

Email victorystacks@gmail.com. Include the task number from the task screen and your iPhone model. We usually reply within a few days. You can also use 'Report a problem' on any task in the app.

FAQ

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

Your progress, photos, voice notes and written notes stay on your phone. The only network activity is a clock check with apple.com and optional Game Center scores. Both are explained in the Privacy Policy.

How do I report an unclear task?

Open the task and tap Report a problem.
