TASK 1001: Privacy Policy

Effective: November 2026

TASK 1001 works offline. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Your progress, photos, voice notes and written notes are stored only on your device. We cannot see them and we do not collect them.

To check that your device clock is right when you complete a challenge, the app asks apple.com for the current time. This request contains none of your data; Apple's server receives the request like any web request, including your IP address and the time. We do not receive it.

Game Center is optional. If you sign in, the app sends Apple two numbers: how many challenges you have completed in total and this month. Apple handles your Game Center data under Apple's privacy policy.

If you use 'Report a problem', your mail app creates an email to us with the reason you chose, any note you write, the task's number, title and details, the app version, and your iOS version and iPhone model. Nothing is sent unless you send the email yourself. We use it only to answer you.

The app is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
